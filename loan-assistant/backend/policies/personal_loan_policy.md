# Personal Loan Policy (PL)

Unsecured personal loans for consumer purposes such as debt consolidation, medical expenses, travel or education.

## 1. Product Limits

**PL-1.1** Loan amount must be between $2,000 and $75,000. Requested terms must be between 12 and 84 months. Requests outside these limits are declined.

**PL-1.2** The loan amount may not exceed 50% of the applicant's gross annual income. Requests above this limit are conditionally approved at the limit.

## 2. Eligibility

**PL-2.1** The minimum gross annual income is $30,000.

**PL-2.2** The minimum credit score for an unsecured personal loan is 620.

## 3. Pricing

**PL-3.1** The base rate for personal loans used in affordability calculations is 11.0% per annum, plus the credit tier adjustment in GCP-7.1.

## 4. Credit Behaviour

**PL-4.1** Because the loan is unsecured, credit utilization above 80% requires a condition to reduce revolving balances, and is declined when combined with any delinquency in the last 24 months.
