# Auto Loan Policy (AL)

Secured vehicle loans. The vehicle is held as collateral.

## 1. Product Limits

**AL-1.1** Loan amount must be between $5,000 and $100,000. Requested terms must be between 24 and 84 months. Requests outside these limits are declined.

## 2. Eligibility

**AL-2.1** The minimum credit score for an auto loan is 600.

**AL-2.2** The minimum gross annual income is $24,000.

## 3. Loan-to-Value

**AL-3.1** The loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the vehicle price. An LTV up to 90% is standard.

**AL-3.2** An LTV above 90% and up to 100% is only approved if the applicant contributes additional down payment so that the loan does not exceed 90% of the vehicle price; this is a condition of approval. An LTV above 100% is an automatic decline.

## 4. Pricing

**AL-4.1** The base rate for auto loans used in affordability calculations is 7.5% per annum, plus the credit tier adjustment in GCP-7.1.
