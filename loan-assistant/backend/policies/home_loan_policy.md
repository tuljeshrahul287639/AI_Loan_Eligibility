# Home Loan Policy (HL)

Secured mortgage loans for the purchase of residential property. The property is held as collateral.

## 1. Product Limits

**HL-1.1** Loan amount must be between $50,000 and $1,000,000. Requested terms must be between 120 and 360 months. Requests outside these limits are declined.

## 2. Eligibility

**HL-2.1** The minimum credit score for a home loan is 650.

**HL-2.2** Applicants must have at least 2 years in current employment (or trading history if self-employed). Shorter tenure requires conditional approval with additional income verification.

**HL-2.3** The minimum gross annual income is $40,000.

## 3. Loan-to-Value

**HL-3.1** The loan-to-value ratio (LTV) is the loan amount divided by the appraised property value. An LTV up to 80% is standard.

**HL-3.2** An LTV above 80% and up to 90% requires mortgage insurance, which becomes a condition of approval. An LTV above 90% is an automatic decline.

## 4. Pricing

**HL-4.1** The base rate for home loans used in affordability calculations is 6.5% per annum, plus the credit tier adjustment in GCP-7.1.

## 5. Affordability

**HL-5.1** For home loans the standard post-loan DTI limit of 43% in GCP-3.1 applies. The DTI limit is never relaxed for secured lending.
