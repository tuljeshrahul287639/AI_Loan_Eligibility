# General Credit Policy (GCP)

Applies to every consumer loan product. Product policies add to, and never relax, these rules.

## 1. Applicant Eligibility

**GCP-1.1** Applicants must be between 21 and 65 years of age (inclusive) at the time of application. Applications outside this range are declined.

**GCP-1.2** Salaried applicants must have at least 1 year in their current employment. Self-employed applicants must have at least 2 years of trading history. Shorter tenure is a risk factor and is escalated to conditional approval at best.

## 2. Credit Bureau Score

**GCP-2.1** Credit score bands: 750 and above is Excellent, 700 to 749 is Good, 650 to 699 is Fair, 600 to 649 is Weak, below 600 is Poor.

**GCP-2.2** A credit score below 600 is an automatic decline for every product. Product policies may set a higher floor.

## 3. Affordability and Debt-to-Income

**GCP-3.1** Post-loan debt-to-income ratio (DTI) is the sum of existing monthly debt payments plus the proposed monthly instalment, divided by gross monthly income. A post-loan DTI up to 43% is within standard appetite.

**GCP-3.2** A post-loan DTI above 43% and up to 50% is outside standard appetite; the loan can only be approved with conditions such as a reduced amount or a longer term. A post-loan DTI above 50% is an automatic decline.

**GCP-3.3** Where the requested amount breaches the 43% DTI limit, the maximum affordable amount at the indicative rate and requested term should be offered as the conditional counter-proposal.

## 4. Credit Behaviour

**GCP-4.1** Revolving credit utilization above 50% is a moderate risk factor; above 80% is a high risk factor.

**GCP-4.2** Delinquencies (30 or more days past due) in the last 24 months: one or two are a risk factor requiring conditions; three or more are an automatic decline.

## 5. Liquidity

**GCP-5.1** Applicants should hold liquid savings of at least 2 times the proposed monthly instalment. Savings below 1 times the instalment are a high risk factor.

## 6. Risk Scoring and Decision Mapping

**GCP-6.1** Each application receives a composite risk score from 0 (lowest risk) to 100 (highest risk), built from credit score, DTI, utilization, delinquency history, employment stability, liquidity and any collateral exposure.

**GCP-6.2** Risk bands: 0 to 35 is Low, 36 to 60 is Medium, above 60 is High.

**GCP-6.3** Decision mapping: Low risk with no policy exceptions is Approved. Medium risk, or any non-fatal policy exception, is Conditionally Approved. High risk, or any automatic-decline rule, is Declined.

## 7. Indicative Pricing for Affordability Calculations

**GCP-7.1** Instalments in affordability checks are calculated at an indicative annual rate equal to the product base rate plus a credit tier adjustment: Excellent +0.0%, Good +1.0%, Fair +2.5%, Weak +4.5%, Poor +7.0%.
