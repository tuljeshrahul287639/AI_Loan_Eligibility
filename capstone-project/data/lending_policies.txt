LENDING POLICIES AND GUIDELINES

1. CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum credit score: 620
- Excellent credit (750+): Priority approval
- Good credit (700-749): Standard approval
- Fair credit (650-699): Requires additional review
- Poor credit (620-649): High scrutiny required

2. DEBT-TO-INCOME (DTI) RATIO
- Maximum acceptable DTI: 43%
- Preferred DTI: Less than 36%
- DTI above 43% with mitigating factors: Requires senior review

3. INCOME REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum monthly income: $30,000
- Income must be documented and verified
- Self-employed applicants require 2 years of tax returns

4. EMPLOYMENT HISTORY
- Minimum 1 year in current position
- Consistent employment pattern preferred
- Job changes within same industry acceptable

5. RISK ASSESSMENT
- Risk score above 70: High risk, requires human review
- Risk score 40-70: Moderate risk, standard procedures
- Risk score below 40: Low risk, faster approval

6. SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS
- Bankruptcy history requires 2+ years since discharge
- Foreclosure requires 3+ years since completion
- Recent inquiries (>3 in 6 months) flag potential credit seeking behavior

7. COLLATERAL AND DOCUMENTATION
- Loan to value ratio considerations
- Property appraisal requirements
- Title insurance and legal documentation mandatory
