LENDING POLICY - RISK ASSESSMENT FRAMEWORK

1. CREDIT RISK SCORING METHODOLOGY
   Credit Score Component: 35% weight
   - 750+ : 95-100 points
   - 700-749 : 80-94 points
   - 650-699 : 60-79 points
   - 600-649 : 40-59 points
   - Below 600 : 0-39 points
   
   Delinquency Penalty: -5 points per delinquency
   Bankruptcy Penalty: -20 points per bankruptcy

2. INCOME STABILITY SCORING
   Income Stability Component: 25% weight
   - Employment length multiplier: years employed x 15
   - Full-time bonus: +15 points
   - Part-time penalty: +5 points only
   - Income-to-loan ratio adjustment
   - Maximum income stability score: 100

3. DEBT RISK SCORING
   Debt Risk Component: 25% weight
   - Base score: 100
   - Debt ratio penalty: debt ratio x 100
   - Recent inquiry penalty: -3 points per inquiry
   - Maximum debt risk score: 100

4. BEHAVIORAL RISK SCORING
   Behavioral Component: 15% weight
   - Recent inquiries: 0-4 inquiries acceptable
   - Inquiry penalty: -3 points per inquiry beyond acceptable
   - Maximum behavioral score: 100

5. OVERALL RISK CALCULATION
   Overall Risk Score = (Credit x 0.35) + (Income x 0.25) + (Debt x 0.25) + (Behavioral x 0.15)
   
   Risk Levels:
   - 75-100: LOW RISK (Approve)
   - 60-74: LOW TO MODERATE RISK (Review)
   - 40-59: MODERATE RISK (Review/Escalate)
   - 25-39: HIGH RISK (Escalate)
   - 0-24: VERY HIGH RISK (Reject)

6. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
   - Auto-Approve: Risk score >= 85 AND Credit score >= 720
   - Auto-Reject: Risk score <= 25 OR Credit score < 620
   - Human Review: All other cases
