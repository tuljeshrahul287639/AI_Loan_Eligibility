LENDING POLICY - CORE REQUIREMENTS

1. MINIMUM CREDIT SCORE
   - Minimum acceptable credit score: 620
   - Excellent credit: 750+
   - Good credit: 700-749
   - Fair credit: 650-699
   - Poor credit: below 650

2. DEBT-TO-INCOME RATIO
   - Maximum acceptable DTI: 43%
   - Recommended DTI: below 36%
   - Critical threshold: 50% (automatic rejection)
   - DTI = (Total Monthly Debt / Monthly Income) x 100

3. INCOME REQUIREMENTS
   - Minimum monthly income: $1,500
   - Stable employment history required: minimum 6 months
   - Acceptable employment types: Full-time, Part-time, Self-employed
   - Income verification: Recent pay stubs or tax returns

4. LOAN ELIGIBILITY
   - Minimum loan amount: $50,000
   - Maximum loan amount: $500,000
   - Loan-to-income ratio: Should not exceed 5x annual income
   - Standard term: 30 years at 6% annual interest rate

5. AGE AND CITIZENSHIP
   - Applicant must be 18 years or older
   - U.S. citizen or permanent resident required
   - Valid government-issued ID required

6. EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION
   - Current employment status must be verified
   - Previous employment history review: minimum 2 years
   - Gap in employment: must be explained
   - Job stability factor considered in approval
