LENDING POLICY - EXCEPTIONS AND SPECIAL CASES

1. BANKRUPTCY HANDLING
   - Discharged bankruptcy: Eligible after 2 years
   - Chapter 7 bankruptcy: Eligible after 7 years
   - Chapter 13 bankruptcy: Eligible if still in repayment
   - Recent bankruptcy (< 2 years): Manual review required
   - Multiple bankruptcies: Automatic rejection

2. CREDIT REPAIR PROGRAMS
   - Recent credit repair (< 6 months): Flag for review
   - Legitimate credit repair: Consider trends
   - Rapid credit score improvement: Verify legitimacy
   - Minimum improvement rate: 20 points annually

3. DELINQUENCY EXCEPTIONS
   - Single delinquency (30 days): May approve with conditions
   - Multiple delinquencies: Manual review
   - Recent delinquency (< 1 year): Increased scrutiny
   - Explained delinquencies: Consider context

4. INCOME EXCEPTIONS
   - Seasonal employment: Annualize income
   - Commission-based income: Use 2-year average
   - Self-employment: Require 2 years tax returns
   - Recent job change (< 3 months): Verify stability

5. DEBT EXCEPTIONS
   - Student loan deferment: May count as $0
   - Medical debt: Consider with context
   - Fraud/disputed debt: Exclude if proving
   - Authorized user accounts: Do not count

6. SPECIAL PROGRAMS
   - First-time homebuyer: DTI limit may increase to 50%
   - Government employee: Standard terms apply
   - Military service: Special consideration available
   - Economic hardship: Case-by-case review

7. MANUAL REVIEW TRIGGERS
   - Confidence score between 50-70%
   - Agent recommendation disagreement
   - Unusual financial patterns
   - Multiple risk factors present
   - Applicant dispute or appeal
